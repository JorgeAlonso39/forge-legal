FORGE Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 16, 2026 · App: FORGE (com.choc2056.gymtracker)

1. Data controller

FORGE is a workout tracking app developed by Choc2056 ("the developer"). For any questions about this policy or your data, contact: [CONTACT-EMAIL] or via GitHub.

2. Data we collect

CategoryDataRequired
AccountEmail address and password (stored encrypted by our authentication provider)Yes
ProfileFull name, username, avatar photo URL, sex, date of birth, height and weightNo (optional)
TrainingExercises, sets, reps, lifted weights, personal records (1RM), workout templatesOnly if you use the feature
Cardio & locationActivity type, duration, distance, calories and precise GPS route (latitude, longitude and timestamp of each point) during cardio sessions you startOnly if you use the feature and grant location permission
Health & measurementsBody weight, body fat and muscle percentageOnly if you use the feature
NutritionLogged foods, target calories and macronutrientsOnly if you use the feature
SocialFriendships, notifications and support reports you submitOnly if you use the feature

The camera is used only to scan food barcodes. No photos or videos are captured, stored or transmitted.

Location is used only in the foreground, while the cardio screen is open. The app never accesses your location in the background.

3. Purpose and legal basis

  • Providing the service (performance of contract, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR): creating your account, storing and displaying your workouts, routes, measurements and nutrition.
  • Personalized calculations (consent, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR): sex, date of birth, height and weight are used only to calculate your target calories and macronutrients. They are optional and can be deleted at any time from your profile.
  • Social features (consent): only if you enable "Appear in the Global Ranking", other users will see your username, avatar and records (1RM, lifted weight and reps). You can disable it at any time in your profile.

We do not use your data for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not perform automated profiling with legal effects.

4. What other users can see

  • Your friends can see your username, full name and avatar.
  • If you enable the Global Ranking, any user can see your username, avatar and strength records. Your email, GPS routes, body measurements and nutrition data are never visible to other users.

The app does not integrate third-party advertising or analytics SDKs (no AdMob, no Google Analytics, no Firebase Analytics).

6. Retention and deletion

Your data is kept while your account is active. You can permanently delete your account and all your data:

  • In the app: Profile → Danger zone → Delete account.
  • On the web: account deletion request.

Deletion is immediate and irreversible: your account, profile, workouts, GPS routes, body measurements, nutrition logs, friendships and notifications are erased. We keep no copies unless legally required.

7. Your rights (GDPR)

You can exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction and portability by writing to the contact email above. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority (in Spain, the AEPD).

8. Minimum age

FORGE is intended for people aged 14 or older. By signing up you confirm you meet the minimum age. If we detect an account belonging to a child under 14 without parental consent, we will delete it.

9. Security

All data is transmitted encrypted over HTTPS/TLS. Database access is protected with Row Level Security policies, so each user can only access their own data.

10. Changes to this policy

If we modify this policy, we will publish the new version at this same address and update the "last updated" date. Substantial changes will be communicated within the app.
