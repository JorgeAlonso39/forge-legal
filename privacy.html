3. Purpose and legal basis

  • Providing the service (performance of contract, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR): creating your account, storing and displaying workouts, routes, measurements, nutrition and Wear sync.
  • Personalized calculations (consent, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR): sex, date of birth, height and weight for target calories/macros. Optional and deletable from your profile.
  • Social features (consent): only if you enable "Appear in the Global Ranking".
  • Product improvement and reliability (legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR): first-party analytics to understand feature usage, detect errors and improve stability. Not used for advertising.
  • Security, abuse and support (legitimate interest / legal obligation where applicable): detect ranking fraud, abuse or Terms violations; suspend or ban accounts; handle support requests.

We do not use your data for third-party advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not perform automated profiling with legal or similarly significant effects without appropriate safeguards.

4. What other users can see

  • Your friends can see your username, full name and avatar.
  • If you enable the Global Ranking, any user can see your username, avatar and strength records. Your email, GPS routes, body measurements, nutrition and analytics events are never visible to other users.
  • A shadow ban may hide or limit your social content for others while your account remains usable for you.

5. Who can access your data

  • Technical processors listed in section 6.
  • Authorized FORGE Ops support/moderation staff, only when needed for security, abuse, Terms enforcement or assistance. Actions are internally audited.

7. Retention and deletion

Account data is kept while your account is active. First-party analytics events may be kept for a limited time for statistics and debugging, then aggregated or deleted. You can permanently delete your account and user data:

  • In the app: Profile → Danger zone → Delete account.
  • On the web: account deletion request.

Deletion is immediate and irreversible for your account, profile, workouts, GPS routes, measurements, nutrition, friendships and notifications. Minimal security/audit records or non-identifiable aggregates may be retained when legally required or for security.

8. Your rights (GDPR)

You can exercise access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction and portability by writing to the contact email above. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority (in Spain, the AEPD).

9. Minimum age

FORGE is intended for people aged 14 or older. By signing up you confirm you meet the minimum age. Accounts of children under 14 without parental consent will be deleted.

10. Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS/TLS. End-user database access is protected with Row Level Security. Operations staff access requires authentication, permissions and, where applicable, multi-factor authentication.

11. Changes to this policy

If we modify this policy, we will publish the new version at this same address and update the "last updated" date. Substantial changes will be communicated in the app when reasonable.
